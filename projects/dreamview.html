DreamView

Digital Comic Viewer

Read comics and images from folders or archives — with a bookshelf, bookmarks, and modes built for manga and webtoons.

Open the archive. Pick up where you left off.

DreamView is built for digital comics and image collections — browse folders or compressed files, manage a library on the bookshelf, and read with controls tuned for long sessions.

Folders and archives

Open image folders or comic archives — CBZ, CBR, CB7, ZIP, RAR, 7z, LHA, ALZ, ISO, and PDF — without unpacking first.

Bookshelf workflow

Recent items, bookmarks, and one-click next/previous book navigation keep large libraries manageable.

Made for how you read

Toggle read direction for Japanese comics, switch single or double pages, and scroll vertically for webtoon-style images.

Archives

Comic formats and compressed files, in one viewer.

DreamView uses The Unarchiver (XADMaster) to read split archives, password-protected files, and non-English filenames — then shows images inside without a separate extract step.

  • CBZ · CBR · CB7 · ZIP · RAR · 7z · LHA · ALZ · ISO
  • PDF and common image formats in folders
  • Encrypted and split-compressed archive support

Reading

Direction, pages, and progress — your way.

Switch between left-to-right and right-to-left reading, single and double-page spreads, and smart view mode that fits each image. Track progress with bookmarks and resume from recent items.

  • Read direction toggle for manga
  • Single / double page and smart view modes
  • Recent items, bookmarks, and next/previous book

Viewing

Zoom, filters, slideshow, and webtoons.

Fullscreen reading, thumbnail browsing, image filters (sharpen, mono, sepia), transition effects, clipboard copy, and vertical scroll for long webtoon pages — with trackpad swipe and zoom support.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add to the bookshelf

    Drop a folder or archive onto DreamView, or browse from the bookshelf to build your library.

  2. 2

    Open and read

    Pick a title, set read direction and page mode, then move through pages with keyboard, trackpad, or on-screen controls.

  3. 3

    Resume later

    Recent items and bookmarks remember where you stopped — jump to the next book in the series with one click.

Development / My Role

I am the author and developer of DreamView for Mac. I design, ship, and maintain the product end to end — archive integration, bookshelf and reading UX, image rendering and filters, localization, and App Store releases.

Built as a native Mac app with Objective-C, AppKit, and The Unarchiver (XADMaster) framework.
